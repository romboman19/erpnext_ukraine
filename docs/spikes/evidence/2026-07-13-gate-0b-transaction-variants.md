## Result

opening Stock Reconciliation.

ERPNext preserves Inventory Dimension through transfer source/target SLE and
through an `update_stock=1` Sales Invoice Return. Stock Reconciliation supports
the dimension only for opening stock and rejects changes to existing
dimensioned stock by design.

## Stock Reconciliation

With no active stock, an opening Stock Reconciliation submitted `5` units at
zero valuation for the same owner. Its SLE carried `tp_spike_lot` and the
dimension balance became `5`.

A second reconciliation attempted to change that owner balance to `3`.
ERPNext raised `ValidationError`, explicitly stating that Stock Reconciliation
with Inventory Dimension is intended solely for opening entries.

This is a platform boundary, not a runner defect. Operational inventory audits
for third-party stock must calculate variances and post controlled Stock Entry
adjustments instead of modifying existing dimension stock with standard Stock
Reconciliation.

## Required implementation rules

1. Ordinary Material Transfer must keep source and target ownership equal.
2. Ownership conversion requires its own authorized workflow and must not be
   disguised as a warehouse transfer.
3. Return documents must preserve the original ownership lot and exact tracked
   Batch/Serial mapping.
4. Standard Stock Reconciliation may initialize dimensioned opening stock only.
5. Operational third-party inventory revisions must post audited variance Stock
   Entries generated by the application.
