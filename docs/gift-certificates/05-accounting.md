# Accounting

Sale at face: Dr cash/bank, Cr paid liability. Discounted face creates paid liability only for money received; promotional funding remains a separate subledger component and becomes expense at redemption. Premium credits configured premium income.

Same-entity redemption: SI payment rows debit paid liability and promotional expense while normal goods revenue remains standard ERPNext. Return SI reverses those accounts. Cross-entity uses settlement receivable payment mapping plus signed issuer/redeemer subledger.

Every active Accounting Profile requires a company Cost Center. Reconciliation compares ledger components, reservations, GL references and settlement pairs.
